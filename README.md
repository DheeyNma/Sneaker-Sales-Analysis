# Sneaker-Sales-Analysis

## Project Overview
This project is an end-to-end data analysis of a sneaker sales dataset, covering data preparation, SQL querying, and interactive dashboard development. The goal was to transform raw transactional data into actionable business intelligence that supports data-driven decision.

## Objective
• Clean and validate raw sales data for accuracy and consistency
• Extract key business insights through structured SQL queries
• Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for executive reporting
• Identify top-performing brands, products, and markets
• Uncover seasonal trends and payment behaviour patterns
• Deliver strategic recommendations to drive revenue growth

## Tools Used
• Microsoft Excel — Data cleaning, deduplication, type validation, and whitespace correction
• SQL — Aggregation, grouping, and querying to extract business insights
• Power BI — Interactive two-page dashboard with visual storytelling and KPIs

## Key Findings
• Total Revenue: $193,100 across 1,000 units sold
• Average Order Value: $547.03
• Top Brand: Nike — $68,000 in total revenue, nearly double second-placed Adidas ($37,000)
• Top Product: Off-White Hoodie and Nike Dunk Low led with approximately 24,000 units each
• Highest Sales Market: Japan leads all geographic markets in total revenue
• Peak Sales Month: July recorded the highest monthly revenue; April was the lowest
• Dominant Payment Method: Wallet payments led at $54,540, with over 80% of transactions cashless\

## Strategic Recommendation
1.  Prioritise Nike and Off-White inventory both consistently lead in volume and revenue
2.  Run targeted promotions in February and April to counteract the seasonal dip
3.  Invest in digital payment infrastructure — 80%+ of transactions are cashless
4.  Build a loyalty programme targeting high-frequency buyers with basket sizes above $200
5.  Scale marketing investment in Japan and use that playbook to activate underperforming markets
6.  Expand streetwear and lifestyle product lines — the top 10 is dominated by hoodies, sneakers, and casual wear
7.  Leverage the July peak with pre-season inventory builds and promotional campaigns
